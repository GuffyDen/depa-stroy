# Home Page Override — ДеПа Строй

This page follows the `ui-ux-pro-max` recommendation of **Exaggerated
Minimalism** and a **Portfolio + Hero-Centric** structure, adjusted to the
approved brand direction.

## Product intent

- The first impression is a premium residential brand, not a contractor.
- The page sells quality of life and architectural value before explaining
  turnkey renovation.
- Visual reference level: contemporary architecture and high-end furniture
  brands, without copying a specific website.

## Typography

- Modern grotesk only; no serif, script, or decorative italic.
- Display: Avenir Next / Helvetica Neue fallback stack.
- UI and body: Helvetica Neue fallback stack.
- Headlines use light-to-regular weights, tight tracking, and generous measure.

## Palette

- Paper: `#F2EEE6`
- Deep paper: `#E7E0D5`
- Sand: `#D3C5B3`
- Ink: `#191A17`
- Graphite: `#22231F`
- Brass: `#A98755`, used sparingly for focus, indices, and active states.

## Layout and rhythm

- 12-column editorial logic expressed through asymmetric CSS grids.
- Spacious density with 112–220px section rhythm.
- Hero remains a strict text/image split with no text over the photograph.
- Portfolio imagery is primary; supporting copy is short.
- No rounded cards, gradients, glass effects, heavy shadows, or decorative
  construction motifs.

## Interaction

- Micro-interactions: 150–300ms.
- Scroll reveals are grouped, subtle, and disabled by reduced-motion.
- All interactive targets are at least 44px high.
- Focus is visible and brass-accented.

## Responsive

- Desktop: editorial asymmetry and wide image fields.
- Mobile: content becomes a single vertical story.
- In the final contact section the form appears before the project leads.
